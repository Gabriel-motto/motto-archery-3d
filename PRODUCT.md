# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Compound-bow archers — club and competition shooters who already own an expensive compound bow and care about protecting it and keeping it stable between ends at the shooting line. Spanish-speaking, reached through word of mouth at competitions and social media. They read gear the way they read their bow: specs, finish, fit.

## Product Purpose

Motto Archery makes a bow stand ("reposa arcos") for compound bows. It was designed by a long-time compound archer for his own bow; other competitors started asking for one, and it is now made to order by 3D printing in many colours (solid colours, two-tone, coloured inserts). The site's job is to make an archer want one and contact the maker to order it.

## Positioning

Designed by a competitor rather than a catalogue accessory; made to order in the colour you choose rather than one stock finish.

## Operating Context

- Landing page used on phones (shared links from WhatsApp/Instagram) and desktop.
- Spanish copy.
- Orders happen by direct contact (WhatsApp/email/Instagram) — no shop or checkout yet; contact channels still pending from the owner.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Real assets: 24 studio photographs of the product (light grey studio background, photographer watermark), brand logo set (arrowhead mark + "MOTTO ARCHERY" wordmark) in red, black and white.
- No 3D model file exists; any 3D rendering must be authored.
- Unknown and must not be invented: price, weight, materials/filament, dimensions, compatibility list, shipping, delivery times, testimonials, sales numbers.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and logo: "Motto Archery", arrowhead mark. Brand red #FF0000 with black and white.
- Tone: precise, competitive, confident; not playful.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product photos and logos only. No testimonials, reviews, press or numbers exist — do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. The product itself is the hero: show the stand, its triangular-cell structure and its colours.
2. Only claim what the photos or the maker's story support.
3. Every path ends in "order yours" by direct contact.
